What we offer

Every kind of workout, all in one place

Whether you want to build muscle, burn fat, dance it out, or recover well — we've got the space, the gear, and the expertise.

Personal Training

One-on-one coaching with certified trainers. Custom programs built around your body and your goals.

Cardio

Full range of treadmills, cycles, ellipticals and cross-trainers to burn fat and build stamina.

Strength Training

Free weights, dumbbells, barbells, plate-loaded machines — everything you need to build serious strength.

Diet Plans

Personalised nutrition plans — up to 17 meal slots a day — designed around your goals, tastes and schedule.

Zumba

High-energy dance workouts in a group setting. Great cardio, even greater vibes.

Steam Bath

Post-workout steam room for recovery, muscle relaxation, and detox after every intense session.
