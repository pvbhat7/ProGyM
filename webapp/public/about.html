About Us

PRO GYM

PRO GYM is a fitness centre located at Kalamba Rd, Dadu Chougule Nagar, Survey Nagar, Kolhapur, Maharashtra 416007. We offer gym memberships with modern strength and cardio equipment, steam, Zumba and personal training, along with workout and diet guidance from our trainers.

PRO GYM was founded by Pranav Patil, a certified fitness coach who leads our team of trainers and personally guides members through their fitness journeys.

What we offer

  • Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and yearly memberships for men and women — see Pricing.
  • Strength training, cardio, steam and Zumba.
  • Personal training and diet plans.
  • The ProGym app at progym.co.in for workout and diet plans, attendance, progress tracking and online membership payments.
